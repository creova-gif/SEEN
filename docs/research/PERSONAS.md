# Personas

**Evidence level.** No user research data exists in the repository. Personas
below are derived from product evidence (roles, onboarding intents, story
catalogue, institutional partner fields, funding component in Figma). Treat
all as **hypotheses to validate** in the first real-user round
(`docs/testing/REAL_USER_TEST_PLAN.md`).

| Persona | Evidence in product | Primary jobs | Key screens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Explorer (viewer)** — reads and listens to community stories, often bilingual | Role `viewer`, intent "Explore culture", EN/FR/ES content | Find meaningful stories; continue later; follow voices | For You, Explore, Library, Search | Hypothesis |
| **Community storyteller (creator)** — individual or collective publishing multi-chapter stories | Role `creator`, intents "Share work"/"Build a body of work", publish wizard, collectives in catalogue | Publish; be discovered; find funding; earn | Creator publish, Profile, Funding | Hypothesis |
| **Community contributor** — adds responses/memories to stories | Intent "Connect with communities", community responses panel | Respond; feel heard | Story reader, responses | Hypothesis |
| **Moderator / cultural steward** | Role `moderator`, moderation governance screen | Keep space safe and respectful | Moderation | Hypothesis |
| **Institutional partner** (museum, archive, broadcaster, school) | `institutionalPartner`, institutional collections, curriculum data in archived catalogue | Present curated collections to an audience | Collections | Hypothesis — partner names in data need confirmation |
| **Funder / programme officer** | Figma Funding organism | Reach eligible storytellers | (future) listing management | Hypothesis, no UI yet |
| **Platform admin (CREOVA)** | Role `admin`, admin dashboard | Operate, approve roles, see health | Admin | Known (internal) |
