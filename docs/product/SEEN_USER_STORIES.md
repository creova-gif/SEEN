# SEEN — user stories (MVP)

**SEEN — a Creova product.** Each story maps to a backlog id (`docs/planning/SEEN_P0_P1_P2_P3_BACKLOG.md`) and to acceptance criteria (`SEEN_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`). Older exploratory list: `docs/ux/USER_STORIES.md`.

## Visitor
- **US-01** As a visitor, I want to understand what SEEN is in one screen, so I can decide whether it's for me. *(P1-01, FB-01)*
- **US-02** As a visitor, I want to open a story without an account, so I can judge the quality first. *(P1-02)*
- **US-03** As a visitor who taps Save, I want a clear prompt to sign up that brings me back to the same story. *(P1-02)*

## Explorer
- **US-10** As an explorer, I want to get to my first story in four steps or fewer. *(P1-03, FB-12)*
- **US-11** As an explorer, I want "Continue" at the top of For You, so I can resume in one tap. *(P1-04, FB-03)*
- **US-12** As an explorer, I want to know why a story is recommended and mute topics I don't want. *(P1-04, FB-14)*
- **US-13** As an explorer, I want to see who made a story and how long it takes. *(P1-05, FB-04, FB-10)*
- **US-14** As an explorer, I want to listen at my own speed and follow along in the text. *(P1-06, FB-05)*
- **US-15** As an explorer, I want a moment of completion when I finish, with the creator's profile and related stories. *(P1-07)*
- **US-16** As an explorer, I want to report a story that's harmful and know what happens next. *(P0-03)*
- **US-17** As an explorer, I want my Library to show Continue, Saved, Following and Collections. *(P1-09)*
- **US-18** As an explorer, I want to search creators and collections, not just stories. *(P1-08)*

## Creator
- **US-20** As a new creator, I want a short intro to what publishing on SEEN involves (review, context, rights). *(P1-12)*
- **US-21** As a creator, I want to edit my profile, including what I'm seeking and how people can support me. *(P1-11, FB-06)*
- **US-22** As a creator, I want a guided publish flow with examples, chips for tags and audience, and a clear next step. *(P1-13, FB-13)*
- **US-23** As a creator, I want to start a story from anywhere in the app. *(D-05, FB-11)*
- **US-24** As a creator, I want to find funding I'm eligible for and track where I've applied. *(done; maintain)*

## Everyone
- **US-30** As a user, I want my account and progress on every device. *(P0-01)*
- **US-31** As a user, I want to read the terms and privacy policy and control my data. *(P0-04, P2-05)*
- **US-32** As a user with low vision, I want visible borders and the option of larger text. *(P1-14, FB-07)*
