## 5. Where Vercel behaviour won

Filters stay in a drawer (wired and accessibility-tested) rather than chips; no "Recommended" chip (no matching engine); no ELIGIBLE tag; captions only for device-voice playback (no invented timing); no readiness percentage; no funder confirmation, panel, interview or grant-agreement screens (SEEN receives no funder data); no real organisation names from Figma placeholders.

## 8. Responsive report

`e2e/audit/ux-audit.spec.ts` sweeps every route, for each role, at 320, 360, 390, 768 and 1280 px and fails on targets under 44 px, controls hidden under the nav, or sideways overflow. All four roles pass, including the new screens. 1440 px was not run separately; 1280 is the widest checked.

## 9. Motion report
