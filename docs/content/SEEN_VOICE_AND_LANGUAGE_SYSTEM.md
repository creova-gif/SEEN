# SEEN voice and language system

SEEN is a storytelling platform for BIPOC voices. It should read like a good editor talking to a curious friend, not like a social network or a campaign.

## Voice in five words
Warm. Plain. Specific. Curious. Respectful.

## Principles
1. **Say what will happen.** Utility copy is utility copy. "Start reading", "Keep reading", "Story saved", "Your story is ready for review". Do not turn buttons, empty states or errors into poetry.
2. **Lead with the story, not the issue.** Catalogue and collection blurbs open with a person, a place or a thing that happens ("Five families tell how they built a life in Canada, in their own words"), not with the problem the story is about.
3. **Make room for all of life.** Joy, memory, migration, family, humour, music, food, history, ordinary days, craft, identity, invention, place, community, achievement. Topic lists, empty states and prompts must not read as hardship-only.
4. **No social-network verbs as slogans.** No "go viral", "trending now", "engagement", "join the conversation", "be seen / get seen". "Follow" stays as a plain action on a creator because people understand it; it is not a growth promise.
5. **Culturally aware, never performative.** No diversity-campaign slogans, no preaching, no "celebrating differences". Name the specific thing (a language, a dish, a neighbourhood, a song).
6. **Short.** One idea per sentence. Prefer verbs and nouns over adjectives.
7. **Accessible language.** Aim for plain-language reading level; avoid idiom that does not translate to French or Spanish; every user-visible string ships in EN, FR and ES.

## "You are now SEEN"
One deliberate brand moment only: the first-visit panel at the top of For You, shown once after sign-up. It is not used in buttons, toasts, empty states or errors. Everywhere else, use the plain version of what happened.

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Story, chapter | content, asset, piece (in UI) |
| Creator | influencer, user (when talking about a storyteller) |
| For You (tab name, kept; see P1-1 in the inventory for a possible rename to Home) | feed |

| Reflections | community voices, engagement |
| Tell a story | Go live, Post |

## Patterns
- **Buttons:** verb plus object ("Save to Library", "Add a reflection", "Next: your interests").
- **Empty states:** one honest sentence about what is missing, one action. "Nothing in progress. Open a story and it will wait for you here."
- **Errors:** what happened, what to do. No blame, no jokes.
- **Success:** the plain fact. "Story saved." "Profile updated."
- **Warnings and content notes:** neutral and specific. "This story includes: grief or loss."
- **Onboarding:** ask one thing per screen, say why we ask. "We use this to pick what you see first."
- **Funding and trust:** never imply a partnership, a guarantee or an outcome SEEN does not control ("the funder decides", "tracked by you").

## Review checklist for any new string
Does it say what happens? Would it still make sense translated? Does it sound like an editor or like a platform? Could it appear in any app (generic) or only in SEEN? Is it true?
